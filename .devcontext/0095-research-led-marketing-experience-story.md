# Advisory record 0095: Research-led marketing experience story

- Date: 2026-10-17
- Status at creation: Research and proposed copy; website unchanged

## Request and context

Michelle rejected the Across Marketing Disciplines presentation in 0094 as a
repackaging of supplied capabilities. She explicitly requested research into how
other senior marketing leaders frame their experience on their own websites to
tell a stronger story. Read 0070, 0072 and 0092–0094 context, current homepage,
existing collaboration offers and leadership-story references.

## Research and interpretation

Reviewed these first-party pages on 2026-10-17:

- Greg Hoffman's Modern Arena: https://www.themodernarena.com/ and /about.
  Leads with strategic/creative leadership and brand, business and people value;
  Nike tenure supplies supporting credibility.
- Fran Hazeldine: https://www.franhazeldine.com/about.
  Connects agency and in-house roles to specific positioning, launches and growth
  examples rather than presenting channels alone.
- Robin Zucker: https://robinzucker.com/about.
  Frames experience through building functions, launching brands and reinventing
  an established brand, supported by examples and self-reported results.
- Kathleen Booth: https://www.kathleen-booth.com/about, also reviewed for a
  point-of-view-led account of agency, CMO and brand/growth experience.

These are examples of framing, not independent verification of their claims or
evidence that a website structure causes better commercial results. Inference:
a clear leadership contribution followed by selective proof would communicate
Michelle's range more strongly than the present mixed channel/lifecycle list.

## Recommendation and draft direction

Propose a heading such as From brand ambition to customer growth. Connect brand
building with acquiring and retaining customer relationships, using agency and
enterprise experience as the distinctive basis of that integrated perspective.
Keep three quiet supporting labels: Brand & positioning; Integrated go-to-market;
Customer lifecycle & growth. Remove the prominent exhaustive channel ribbon in
any subsequently authorized implementation; retain detailed disciplines as
supporting evidence where relevant in stories rather than inventing results.

Draft language should explain how her breadth influences decisions and leadership,
not assert she is a CMO or solely authored named campaigns. Existing HSBC/RBC
stories can substantiate this framing with their approved contribution wording.
Preserve the other experience dimensions unless requested otherwise.
