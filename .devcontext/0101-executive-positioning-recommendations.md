## Recommendations proposed

1. Keep the personal hero headline, but make the opening paragraph more concrete
   about building brands, modernizing marketing and leading teams through change.
   Position marketing leadership, growth and reinvention as the main promise.
2. Explain why agency plus brand-side experience changes Michelle's judgment:
   combining customer understanding and creative ambition with the ability to
   align people and execute within complex organizations. Breadth should support
   that contribution rather than function as a stand-alone inventory.
3. Add compact evidence to Leadership in Action: Michelle's role, what changed
   and a verified outcome. Preserve personal lessons; do not rely on reflections
   or films alone to establish executive impact. Metrics are welcome only when
   real and shareable; concrete qualitative outcomes can also be useful.
4. Make people leadership tangible with an example of alignment, changed ways of
   working or capability built. Formal change-management practitioner roles may
   require methodologies/certification not established by the site. Marketing
   leadership through transformation is a better-supported central positioning
   than claiming all specialist change roles.

Suggested opening copy is a proposal for review, not a new published claim:
“I'm Michelle Ching. I help organizations build stronger brands, modernize
marketing and lead teams through change—connecting customer insight, creative
ambition and commercial discipline to create meaningful growth.”
