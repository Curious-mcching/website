# Work record 0104: Executive journey and offering review

- Date: 2026-10-17
- Status at creation: Advisory only; recommendations not yet implemented

## Request and review

Following the implementation in [0103](0103-executive-positioning-and-agile-leadership.md),
Michelle asked what other areas across the website could be more compelling and
executive-like. Reviewed the current page, offerings, thought leadership data,
metadata and contact/footer. Rechecked first-party Arc'teryx Global Brand Operations
VP and Genea Marketing VP postings. They support the importance of commercial
judgment, team capability and cross-functional execution, but do not prescribe
website design. The design/flow recommendations below are our judgment, not an
employer survey finding.

## Recommendations for discussion

1. Change the single hero CTA from Explore my thinking (#challenger) to See
   leadership in action (#experience). This places demonstrated experience ahead
   of the emerging thought-leadership/writing framework for interested hiring
   leaders and clients. Preserve the personal headline, one primary hero CTA,
   Discover the throughline link and thought-leadership navigation.
2. Replace the fourth collaboration offering, Challenger conversations, with
   Leadership & change. Current developing-space/future-collaboration language is
   less grounded than the other three offerings. The newly confirmed agile example
   supplies credible experience for team alignment, ways of working and capability
   building. Keep the invitation to fellow thinkers in thought leadership/contact.
   Do not position this as formal change-management certification or services
   already delivered to advisory clients.
3. Strengthen the commercial decision in existing stories: opportunity prioritized,
   choice/tradeoff made, alignment and investment judgment, and observable change.
   These require actual facts; do not invent allocation authority, market/segment
   choices, budgets or outcomes to make the narrative sound senior.
4. Tighten repeated future-focused framing around thought leadership/writing;
   keep the three pillars and truthful empty publication state. A first short POV
   grounded in the agile example could articulate an executive view of how
   marketing transformation connects strategy, ownership and ways of working.
   This is a proposed future writing topic, not a completed/published paper.
5. Consider a discreet Request my executive profile email link in contact so
   recruiters can request a suitable profile without publishing the existing resume
   and company performance figures. Do not expose/download the resume or send it
   automatically. Any public bio/metadata should stay consistent with marketing,
   growth and transformation leadership and the established scope of experience.

Prioritize the hero destination and fourth offering, which can be implemented
with existing verified facts and make the business contribution clearer. Preserve
curiosity, warmth and the personal audience; executive presence should come from
clarity and evidence rather than a formal tone or more decorative sections.
