# Advisory record 0107: Tasteful visual polish opportunities

- Date: 2026-10-17
- Status at creation: Recommendations only; application unchanged

## Request and review

Michelle asked where else tasteful modern, sophisticated touches would improve
the site after the collaboration horizon treatment. Reviewed current source,
0099 and 0100–0106 context, and live desktop thought-leadership, writing, contact
and leadership-story sections. Used a temporary QA tab and closed it. No external
trend claims or research-based engagement claims are made; recommendations are
design judgment grounded in the current page.

## Recommendations

1. Highest priority: replace the three small boxed thought-leadership diagrams
   with a coherent family of bespoke, unboxed illustrations. Each should express
   its topic (challenging a pattern, human/machine connection, evolving through
   change) with related palette/materials and restrained scale. Avoid unrelated
   decorative renders and the rejected ribbons, dots and sharp-angle motifs.
2. Refine collaboration cards with smaller quieter numerals, considered spacing
   and subtle palette-based hover/focus emphasis. Let typography and hierarchy
   carry their sophistication rather than adding four large images.
3. Add editorial polish within expanded leadership stories: stronger opening
   hierarchy and selectively bring an existing real campaign still forward near
   the contribution summary. Use actual work only where available, keep attribution
   and existing evidence, and avoid inventing visual proof for the other stories.
4. As already discussed in 0105, one authentic supplied portrait would add a
   personal counterpoint in the introductory narrative. Exact crop/placement
   requires the real supplied photo; no generated likeness or photo request is
   necessary for this advisory answer.

Preserve the strong abstract hero, integrated horizon, current writing image and
contact composition. Contact already has a strong color field, oversized typography,
fine curves and circular CTA; additional illustration is lower priority. Start
with the thought-leadership illustration family. No need to decorate every gap.

Desktop live inspection only; not a new mobile/accessibility audit. No application
